## Brand & Style

This design system serves a curated culinary collective bridging artisanal pantry goods, ready-to-heat chef provisions, and bespoke catering services. The brand communicates understated luxury, culinary authenticity, and approachable refinement. 

The visual style unites **Warm Modern Minimalism** with **Tactile Editorial Craft**. It avoids cold tech tropes and fussy rustic clichés, adopting instead the ambiance of an architect-designed European gourmet larder: generous open air, unhurried typography, natural linen surfaces, and subtle, physical paper finishes. Every interface interaction feels deliberate, welcoming, and tactile—reassuring clients whether they are securing a quick pantry staple or curating a multi-course private event.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in kitchen materiality—clay cookware, preserved herbs, natural grain, and cast iron. 

- **Primary (`#9E3D24` — Roasted Auburn / Terracotta):** Anchors primary calls to action, brand highlights, active navigation nodes, and purchase triggers.
- **Secondary (`#4A5A3F` — Warm Olive Sage):** Conveys freshness, organic pantry provenance, seasonal availability tags, and chef curation badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E4A853` — Buttery Saffron):** Reserved for star ratings, special seasonal notices, awards, and delicate hover flourishes.
- **Neutral (`#1E1E1C` — Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded ink tone ensuring high-contrast, effortless legibility for long-form culinary descriptions and ingredient lists.
- **Canvas & Containers:** Base backgrounds utilize a dual-tone warm foundation. Page canvas rests on Linen `#FBF9F5`, transitioning to `#F3EFEA` for card surfaces, drawer panels, and structural alternating bands.
- **Subtle Linework (`#E5DFD7`):** Defines clean, muted hairline dividers and quiet card boundaries without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of **Playfair Display** with the clean, approachable legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Editorial Headings:** Playfair Display introduces rhythm and poise to recipe narratives, meal categories, and brand introductions. It features tighter negative letter spacing at display scales to maintain cohesion.
- **Functional Interface & Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures crisp legibility for ingredient specs, allergen sheets, weight metrics, pricing, and checkout actions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Category tags, dietary chips, and preparation timestamps use uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) to anchor technical details within lush culinary imagery.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a responsive 12-column grid designed with an editorial magazine sensibility. Content avoids edge-to-edge clutter, relying on generous horizontal borders and clear breathing room.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and a controlled container max-width of `1360px`, framed by `3rem` canvas margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` page margins. Cards collapse into two-column pairings; category navigations switch to horizontal scroll trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) side margins. Menus transform to sliding full-bleed overlays, and multi-tier pantry filters collapse into an off-canvas drawer.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous inter-section buffers (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` for hero and feature transitions) allow photography of artisan meals and pantry goods to stand proud.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews synthetic high-blur dropshadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **fine hairline framing**:

- **Ground (Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` serves as the warm foundation.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Interactive Cards & Lists):** Pure `#F3EFEA` backed by an ultra-subtle tinted perimeter border: `1px solid #E5DFD7`. 
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Modals, Cart Drawers, Dropdowns):** Solid `#FBF9F5` elevated with a warm, diffuse ambient shadow tinted with espresso charcoal: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 30, 28, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 30, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Card Hover Response:** Interactive items do not lift abruptly; they deepen via subtle hairline shift (`#D1C7BC`) and an organic warm shadow transition: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(158, 61, 36, 0.07)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry, balancing culinary warmth with contemporary architectural order:

- **Base Elements:** Buttons, text fields, badges, and small controls use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards, Meal Tiles, and Drawers:** Feature `1rem` (16px) corner radiuses (`rounded-lg`), producing soft organic silhouettes that complement food photography.
- **Hero Banners & Feature Panels:** Utilize `1.5rem` (24px) corner rounding (`rounded-xl`) to feel like welcoming framed platters.
- **Pill Accents:** Specific micro-components such as dietary pills (`Gluten-Free`, `Organic`) and counter badges leverage full rounded pill capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#9E3D24` with `#FBF9F5` text, 0.5rem border radius, medium font weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), with padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts to a deep auburn `#85321D`.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #4A5A3F`, text in `#4A5A3F`. Hover fills with subtle tint `rgba(74, 90, 63, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Transparent background, underlined text in `#1E1E1C` with an animated hover line in `#9E3D24`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact capsules (`padding: 0.375rem 0.875rem`, border-radius: full).
- **Dietary (Vegan, Nut-Free):** `#F3EFEA` background, `1px solid #E5DFD7`, text `#4A5A3F` in `label-sm`.
- **Selected Filters:** Filled `#4A5A3F` with clean white text.

### Product & Meal Cards
- Background `#F3EFEA`, border `1px solid #E5DFD7`, radius `1rem`.
- Imagery is framed with an internal inset ratio of 4:3 or 1:1, soft corners (`0.75rem`), and subtle warm tone grading.
- Content zone separates title (`Playfair Display`), concise serving details, dietary icons, and an actionable "+ Add to Cart" or "Configure Order" trigger.

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors (Catering & Ordering)
- Background `#FBF9F5`, border `1.5px solid #E5DFD7`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, text `#1E1E1C`.
- Focus state activates a clean `#9E3D24` ring (`1.5px`) with zero harsh offsets.
- Date and time pickers for meal prep delivery feature distinct calendar tiles styled in olive and linen.

### Lists & Ingredient Breakdowns
- Alternating subtle rows or clean separated lines bordered by `#E5DFD7`.
- Primary item titles sit in `body-md` bold, with weight/origin subtext in `body-sm` muted charcoal (`#63615C`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom 20px inputs with 4px corner radius (checkboxes) or full circle (radios).
- Default: `1.5px solid #D1C7BC` on `#FBF9F5`.
- Checked: Solid `#9E3D24` with crisp white SVG checkmarks or inner dot.

### Specialized Catering Inquirer & Portion Steppers
- **Portion Steppers:** Rounded pill container with `#E5DFD7` border, containing `-` and `+` action icons surrounding an espresso charcoal count.
- **Catering Tier Cards:** Distinctive three-tier pricing cards showcasing curated banquet menus with editorial script flourishes, subtle gold star icons (`#E4A853`), and dedicated inquiry triggers.
